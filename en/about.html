Overview

I am a computer engineer and researcher focused on algorithms and artificial-intelligence models for time series, satellite imagery and scientific data. My work combines remote sensing with deep learning, including wavelet transforms integrated into convolutional neural networks.

I have taken part in research projects that automate the analysis of astronomical and geospatial data, building systems that improve the detection and characterization of natural and astrophysical phenomena. That work covers predictive models, scientific databases and large-scale processing with Python, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, GDAL, Google Earth Engine and SQL.

I want to keep applying artificial intelligence and data analysis to complex systems, and to build precise, reproducible tools with a clear scientific and technical impact.
