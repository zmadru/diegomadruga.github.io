CCT detector — cycles, change and trend

Research project that applies a Cycle-Change-Trend (CCT) detector to remote-sensing raster time series. The method combines temporal analysis, Fourier transforms and heuristic rules to classify pixels as cyclic, changing or trending.


  • Python
  • NumPy
  • MODIS
  • GDAL
  • Time-series analysis
  • Fourier transforms
