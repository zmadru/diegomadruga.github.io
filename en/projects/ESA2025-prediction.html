ESA Living Planet Symposium, Vienna 2025 — Prediction

Poster presented at the ESA Living Planet Symposium in Vienna in 2025 on forecasting techniques for time series. Automatic algorithms downloaded image-level time series for the study area, and the data were then processed to produce forecasts with ARIMA models.
